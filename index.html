Mechanical Engineering · Robotics

Yuelong Zhang

I am an undergraduate researcher in Mechanical Engineering at Zhejiang University and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

My research explores how bio-inspired elastic structures and learning-based control can work together in embodied robotic systems. I am particularly interested in compliant actuation, human-like bipedal locomotion, and reinforcement learning for robots whose mechanisms actively shape their dynamics.
