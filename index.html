Yuelong Zhang

Undergraduate Researcher · Mechanical Engineering · Robotics

I am an undergraduate researcher in Mechanical Engineering at Zhejiang University and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

Research Keywords:

  • 🦿 bipedal locomotion
  • ⚙️ compliant mechanisms
  • 🧠 robot learning

My research focuses on bio-inspired elastic structures and learning-based control for embodied robotic systems. I study compliant actuation, human-like bipedal locomotion, and reinforcement learning for robots whose mechanisms actively shape their dynamics.
